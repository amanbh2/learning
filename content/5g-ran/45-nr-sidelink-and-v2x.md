# NR Sidelink & V2X (PC5 Interface)

## Overview
NR Sidelink (SL) enables direct device-to-device (D2D) communication over the PC5 interface without going through the base station. V2X (Vehicle-to-Everything) is the primary use case. This note covers NR SL architecture, resource allocation modes, and comparison with LTE V2X.
