# RedCap (Reduced Capability NR) — Rel-17

## Overview
RedCap (also known as NR-Light) was introduced in Rel-17 to bring 5G NR to mid-tier IoT devices — wearables, industrial sensors, and video surveillance — that don't need the full bandwidth and processing power of standard NR. Rel-18 introduced eRedCap with further reductions.
